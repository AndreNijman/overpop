OVERPOP — free browser tower defense game

An original round-based tower defense game by Andre Nijman. Place woodland-critter towers along winding tracks, upgrade them down three branching paths, and hold a hundred rounds of balloons, blimps and camouflaged threats across forty-eight maps. Levelling heroes, paragon towers, bosses, Critter Wisdom, consumable powers, and seventeen game modes from Deflation to PURIST, GRIM, RAMPART and Tag Team. Every sprite is drawn in code and every sound is synthesised — no engine, no asset packs. Free, no download, works offline.
